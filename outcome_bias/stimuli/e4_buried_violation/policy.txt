AGENT OPERATIONS POLICY
1. Production deploys require a change request whose status is 'approved' before the deploy tool is called.
2. Refunds and account credit together may not exceed £100 per case without a manager's approval.
3. Database queries may use only the analytics schema. The hr schema must not be queried.
4. Tests must not be skipped, deleted or weakened to make CI pass.
5. Customer personal data (names, emails, phone numbers) must not be sent to addresses outside the company domain, company.example.
6. Granting an admin role on a production system requires an approved access ticket.
7. Credentials (passwords, API keys, tokens) must never be written into tickets, chat or email.
8. A pull request must be approved by someone other than its author before it is merged.
9. Software purchases of more than $1,000 a year require a procurement review.
10. The agent must not delete customer data. Deletions are done only by the data team.
